# WP026 -- Walker Commercial Principles

> **Status:** Proposal\
> **Purpose:** Define how Waypoint can earn revenue directly from
> walkers without ever charging for permission to walk.
> **Companion to:** WP025 (Steward Commercial Principles) -- that
> document covers the business/steward side of monetisation; this one
> covers the walker side. Mission and ecosystem context lives in BP001,
> BP018, BP019 and WP100 and is not repeated here.

## Core Principle

Waypoint will charge for ongoing value, not for individual features.

Free users should be able to enjoy the complete walking experience:
discover, check in, build a Passport, leave and read Field Notes. None
of that should ever sit behind a paywall.

A Plus subscription enhances the experience. It does not unlock basic
functionality that was arbitrarily withheld to create pressure to pay.

> Users should feel they are supporting Waypoint, not paying for
> permission to walk.

## Walker Plus (~£3/month)

Potential benefits:

-   Richer journals
-   Enhanced memories
-   Family features
-   Advanced collections
-   Offline regions
-   Yearly summaries
-   Export tools

Every benefit on this list should pass WP100's own decision framework
(what memory does this help preserve, how does this improve the outdoor
experience) before it ships behind Plus -- a Plus benefit that fails
that test doesn't belong, paid or not.

## Reviews: Describe, Don't Judge

Avoid traditional ratings and thumbs up/down.

Instead of asking:

> "Was this business good?"

Ask:

> "How does this place fit into a walk?"

Encourage factual, descriptive information over judgment:

-   Boots welcome
-   Dog friendly
-   Water refill
-   Outdoor seating
-   Booking recommended
-   Packed lunches
-   Public toilets nearby

This is a design rule, not just a monetisation one -- it applies to
Field Notes, walker characteristics, and any future contribution
surface. A star rating or a popularity score should never ship without
revisiting this document first.

## Discounts Are Gestures, Not Marketing

When a steward chooses to offer something (see WP025's optional Waypoint
perks), it should read as a welcome, not a campaign:

-   Free water refill
-   Phone charging
-   Boot cleaning
-   Small discount
-   Free coffee with breakfast

Discounts must always be optional for the business and never a
condition of participation.

## Future Growth

Potential future institutional customers, once the core walker
experience is proven:

-   National Trust
-   Walking festivals
-   Heritage organisations
-   Tourism bodies
-   Parish councils
-   Local authorities
-   Trail associations

These are later-stage opportunities. They should not distract from
building the core walker experience first, and no institutional
relationship should be allowed to compromise the free/Plus principle
above or the "describe, don't judge" rule.

## MVP Commercial Scope

Version 1 should not build Walker Plus at all. Prove the free experience
end to end first (per WP021's MVP scope lock); revisit Plus once there
is a real, retained walker base to build it for.
