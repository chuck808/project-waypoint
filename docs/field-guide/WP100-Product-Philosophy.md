# WP100 -- Product Philosophy

> **Purpose:** Explain why Waypoint exists and provide a product compass
> for every future decision.

## Waypoint is not a hiking app

Waypoint uses maps, routes and GPS, but those are tools---not the
purpose.

The purpose of Waypoint is to help people build a lasting relationship
with places.

## Our Product Promise

Technology should quietly step aside and allow the world to be noticed.

If a feature increases time spent looking at the phone instead of the
landscape, it should be challenged.

## Product Pillars

### Notice

Encourage observation over consumption.

### Remember

Preserve stories, not just statistics.

### Return

Help people rediscover meaningful places over a lifetime.

### Share

Share experiences with people who matter, not algorithms.

## Decision Framework

Every feature proposal should answer:

-   What memory does this help preserve?
-   How does this improve an outdoor experience?
-   What unnecessary complexity does it avoid?
-   Will this still feel appropriate in ten years?

## Anti-goals

Waypoint will not optimise for: - addictive engagement - vanity
metrics - infinite feeds - unnecessary notifications - collecting data
for commercial exploitation
