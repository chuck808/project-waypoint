# Field Guide 001 – Surfaces

Every Waypoint surface should answer one question:

> What kind of place am I?

## Walker App

Feels like a companion walking beside you.

- personal
- quiet
- encouraging
- observational
- warm

## Public Website

Feels like the doorway of a welcoming café.

It invites rather than sells.

## Business Portal

Feels like opening the café each morning.

It helps a business understand how it is becoming part of walkers' journeys.

## Admin Portal

Feels like looking after a village.

It is stewardship, not control theatre.
